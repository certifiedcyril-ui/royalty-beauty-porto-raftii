# Royal Beauty Boutique

Create a stunning luxury website for a hair salon boutique called 'Royalty Beauty by Ikonomaki' in Porto Rafti, Athens Greece.

Hero section with headline 'Η Πολυτέλεια που Σου Αξίζει' and online booking button.

Services section showing: haircut, hair makeover, hair extensions, makeup, eyelashes — each with elegant description.

Online booking form for appointments — name, service, preferred date and time, phone number.

Reviews section showing 4.6/5 stars from 83 Google reviews with testimonials.

About us section describing a luxury boutique hair salon experience.

Opening hours section: Monday: Κλειστά Tuesday-Friday: 11:00 - 19:00 Saturday: 11:00 - 18:00 Sunday: Κλειστά

Contact section with phone number 2299074486 and location in Porto Rafti Athens.

Location map.

Footer with phone number, Instagram and social media links.

Strict luxury design — black white and gold colors only. Elegant typography. Lots of white space. Premium boutique feel.

NO AI generated face or hand images. Use abstract elegant placeholders.

Everything in Greek. Make it look like a €5000 website.
